Internship

Today we worked from home for our internship. Our assignment is to update the old web platform they use and add new features. Bert started on improving an existing part that consists of very bad code. I started writing a new feature. I'm going to build a kind of exchange platform where G2Studio can swap files with their clients, a kind of Dropbox in your web browser.

We don't really know the office hours yet, so we worked from 10:00 to 18:00, which seemed reasonable to me.

Tomorrow morning we have to pick up our residence permit. The residence permit means we can actually work at the office with our visas, legally. It also gives us a multiple-entry visa, which means that while my visa is valid I can leave and re-enter the country. We were thinking of maybe going to Hong Kong, so with our multiple-entry visa we can.
