On Tuesday a few of us went out to eat at a well-known seafood restaurant. It was really good. Once again I got to know lots of things I'd never try at home! It was definitely worth it.

Beer tasting

Like every week, we went to the beer tasting at De Refter again. We bring more and more people along! This time we also brought our Chinese colleague. She saw the photos I took every week and really wanted to come. The more the merrier, right?

This week it was 4 Japanese beers and 2 Italian beers. Another really good week!

I also went to the Shanghai auto show, but that's for the next blog post! On Friday I'm going out to eat with the people from my company, so that'll be fun too.
